An American 4th of July Weekend Adventure

Meet Asha TC²

Asha is a hardworking student and tech professional who has been grinding through certifications, sprints, and on-call shifts. This 4th of July weekend, she finally clocks out, loads her suitcase onto the roof rack of her trusty red road-trip car, and hits the open American highway.

Goal: Drive to the vacation destination before sunset. Collect souvenirs, snacks, and postcards along the way — and steer (or hop!) over potholes, traffic cones, roadblocks, and low-fuel hazards along the road.
